## **Case Study: UPI / Online Payment Fraud**



##### UPI (Unified Payments Interface) fraud is a common cybercrime in which attackers trick individuals into authorizing fraudulent transactions through mobile payment apps. This type of fraud exploits user trust and lack of awareness rather than technical system vulnerabilities.

##### 

##### A typical case begins when a victim receives a phone call, SMS, or message via platforms like WhatsApp from someone posing as a bank official, delivery agent, or customer. The fraudster claims that money needs to be “credited” to the victim’s account and sends a “collect request” on a UPI app. Step-by-step, the attacker: 

##### (1) builds urgency or trust

##### (2) sends a payment request instead of transferring money

##### (3) instructs the victim to enter their UPI PIN to “receive” funds

##### (4) once the PIN is entered, the money is debited from the victim’s account.

##### &#x20;In some cases, fake QR codes are also used to mislead victims.

##### 

##### The most common targets include elderly individuals, new smartphone users, small business owners, and people unfamiliar with digital payments. However, even educated users can fall victim due to social engineering tactics.

##### 

##### The consequences can be severe, including immediate financial loss, emotional stress, and reduced trust in digital systems. Recovery of funds is often difficult, especially if the fraud is not reported quickly. This highlights the importance of awareness—users should remember that entering a UPI PIN always authorizes a payment, not a receipt.
