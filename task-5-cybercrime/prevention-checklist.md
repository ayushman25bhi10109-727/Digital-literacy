### Stay Safe Online: Checklist for College Students in India



* #### Use strong, unique passwords 

###### &#x20;     for every account (combine letters, numbers, and symbols). Avoid using names, birthdays, or common words.

* #### Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)

###### &#x20;     on email, social media, and banking apps for an extra layer of security.

* #### Never share OTPs, passwords, or UPI PINs

###### &#x20;     with anyone—even if they claim to be from your bank or a trusted services 

* #### Verify links before clicking

###### &#x20;    —phishing messages often mimic colleges, banks, or popular platforms. Check the sender and URL carefully.

* #### Download apps only from official stores

###### &#x20;     like Google Play Store or Apple App Store to avoid malware.

* #### Be cautious on public Wi-Fi

###### &#x20;    —avoid logging into banking or sensitive accounts when using campus or café networks.

* #### Regularly update your phone and apps

###### &#x20;     to fix security vulnerabilities and stay protected against new threats.

* #### Review app permissions

###### &#x20;     and deny access to unnecessary data like contacts, location, or storage.



## UPI / Financial Safety Tips:



###### Remember: Entering your UPI PIN means you are sending money, not receiving it.

###### Never approve “collect requests” from unknown sources.

###### Do not scan unknown QR codes\*\* or accept payment requests from strangers, especially for “refunds” or “prizes.”
